Privacy Isn't a Feature. It's Our Foundation.

Unlike traditional cloud budgeting apps, pennyBook Ledger never uploads your financial ledger to remote databases.

🚫 Zero Internet Access

The application does not declare android.permission.INTERNET. The Android OS prevents the app from opening network sockets or uploading telemetry.

🔐 SQLCipher 256-Bit Encryption

All database records are protected by SQLCipher 4.17.0 and hardware-backed keys managed securely inside the Android Keystore.

🛡️ Cloud Backup Exclusion

Explicit OS data extraction rules ensure your private SQLite database and keys are never uploaded to automated Google Drive cloud backups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how to get the most out of pennyBook Ledger's offline tools.

How does pennyBook Ledger protect my data offline? +
All financial entries are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database encrypted via SQLCipher 4.17.0. The application does not request internet access, ensuring zero external data transmission.
How do I back up and restore my records? +
Navigate to Settings → Backup & Restore. You can create an encrypted .pennybook backup protected by a personal password using Argon2id and AES-256-GCM, or export an unencrypted SQLite .db snapshot. Files are saved directly to your chosen device folder via Android's Storage Access Framework.
Can I change currency and number formats? +
Yes. Open Settings → Currency & Numbering. You can select your preferred ISO 4217 currency (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, PKR, BDT, etc.) and toggle between Indian Numbering (Lakhs & Crores) and International Numbering (Millions & Billions). Stored records retain their exact numerical balance without accidental exchange rate conversion.
What is the Udhaar Ledger? +
The Udhaar Ledger allows you to track personal debts and receivables (money lent to or borrowed from others), record partial repayments, monitor bank EMI dates, and schedule battery-friendly notification reminders.
How does CSV import and template download work? +
From Backup & Restore → Import from CSV, you can download an empty CSV template or a realistic sample CSV directly onto your device. Before importing, the app displays a full pre-import validation report with duplicate detection.
